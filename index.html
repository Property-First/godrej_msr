ABOUT GODREJ MSR CITY PHASE 2

Godrej MSR City Phase 2 Project Overview

Godrej MSR City Phase 2 is the upcoming phase of Godrej MSR City by Godrej Properties in Shettigere, North Bangalore. The new phase is planned with 805+ residences across 3 high-rise towers, with a 2B + G + 15-floor structure and spacious 2 & 3 BHK apartments.

Phase 2 forms part of the larger Godrej MSR City integrated township, planned with approximately 4,000 homes across phases. The township is designed around extensive landscaped areas, with approximately 20 acres of green space, around 70% open space and a 1.5 lakh sq. ft. clubhouse.

Located on BK Halli Road, Shettigere, the development provides access to NH 44, the upcoming Doddajala Metro Station, GODREJ MSR CITY PHASE 2 Tech Cloud and Kempegowda International Airport. The supplied reference also confirms Shettigere as the project location and 2 & 3 BHK residences as the core configurations.
